Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Zero-Log Memory Proxy Streaming Policy

At VidLand, we take your privacy with absolute seriousness. Unlike traditional online converters, VidLand processes media streams strictly in RAM memory buffers during active transfers. No downloaded media, video URLs, or user IP addresses are ever written to server disk or persistent databases.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration, email addresses, or payment details. We do not track personal identifying information (PII) or build user advertising profiles.

3. Local Browser Storage Usage

VidLand uses client-side HTML5 LocalStorage strictly for theme state preferences (dark mode vs. light mode) and your recent search history. This data remains on your physical device and can be cleared at any time directly in your browser settings or via our interface.

4. Compliance & Contact

VidLand adheres to global data privacy principles including GDPR and CCPA. For privacy inquiries, please contact our privacy compliance team at support@vidland.app.
