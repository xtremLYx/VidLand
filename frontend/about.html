About VidLand

Video downloads, refined and secure.

Our Mission

VidLand was founded with a singular purpose: to replace clunky, ad-riddled online converters with a museum-grade, museum-clean web app for content creators, educators, and video enthusiasts worldwide.

Engineering Principles

  • Ultra HD 4K & 1080p Stream Processing: Extract full original media streams up to 60fps.
  • High Bitrate MP3 Extraction: Convert YouTube videos into 320kbps audio files in seconds.
  • In-Memory Streaming Proxy: Zero persistent server storage, protecting user privacy.
  • Apple-Caliber Aesthetic: Clean typography, smooth dark mode, and zero invasive popups.
